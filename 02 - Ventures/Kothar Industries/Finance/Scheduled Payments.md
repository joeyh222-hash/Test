### 股東往來 / 還股往 System
The accountant set up a structure where income from Dad's cases and projects is booked as **loans from shareholders (股東往來)** rather than company revenue. This builds up a balance that the family can draw down over time as a monthly allowance — recorded as 還股東往來 (repaying the shareholder loan).

- Withdrawals appear as **現金支出 — 還股往** in the transaction log
- Typical cadence: **NT$40,000/month** or **NT$80,000 every 2 months** to Mom
- The balance available to withdraw depends on how much has been booked as 股東往來 income
- This is not an operating expense — it's a drawdown of pre-existing shareholder loan balance
