## What It Is
FOLIO is an AI-powered essay grading platform built specifically for Taiwan's GSAT (大學學測) English composition. It automates essay evaluation while staying aligned with official exam standards — delivering detailed, personalized feedback reports back to cram schools and teachers within 3–5 business days.

**Core mission:** *"Let each student receive the precise feedback that previously only a few could access."*

## The Problem It Solves
Teachers don't have time to give meaningful feedback on dozens of essays. Students need detailed, exam-standard guidance to improve. FOLIO handles the mechanical grading so teachers can focus on teaching.

> "A teacher's passion and empathy cannot be automated, but mechanical grading can." — Joey Huang, Co-founder

---

## Target Customers
- **Cram schools (補習班)** — streamline workflow, differentiate service offering
- **English teachers** — reclaim time from mechanical grading
- **High school students** — detailed, individualized feedback
- **Parents** — tangible progress documentation

---

## How It Works
1. Schools scan and submit student essays
2. FOLIO digitizes, grades, and generates personalized reports
3. Reports returned within **3–5 business days**
4. No software installation or workflow changes required for clients

---

## Service Tiers

### FOLIO GSAT (Standard)
- ~20-page personalized learning guide per student
- Introductory price: **NT$69–99/report** (regular: NT$89–119)
- Includes:
  - Scoring breakdown
  - Line-by-line corrections
  - Revision suggestions
  - Vocabulary upgrades
  - Practice problems
  - AI detection analysis

### FOLIO GSAT Lite
- ~5–8 page streamlined report
- Introductory price: **NT$29–39/report** (regular: NT$39–49)
- Includes: scoring breakdown, corrections, feedback (no extended materials)

## Grading Criteria (Official GSAT Standards)
| Category     | Points |
|--------------|--------|
| Content      | 0–5    |
| Organization | 0–5    |
| Grammar      | 0–5    |
| Vocabulary   | 0–5    |

---

## Pricing Model
- No contracts, monthly fees, or minimums
- Batch-based pricing tiers: 1–50 / 51–150 / 151–400 / 400+ essays
- **Free trial: up to 20 essays**

---

## Key Differentiators
- Official GSAT exam-standard alignment (not generic AI feedback)
- Taiwan-focused design and pedagogy
- Zero operational burden on client schools
- Consistent, cross-teacher evaluation standards
- Long-term student progress tracking capability
