# FOLIO — Pricing Reference

## Core Pricing Rules

- Billing unit is always "per student report," never "per essay"
- Pricing tier is determined per batch, not monthly cumulative volume
- First paid batch gets introductory discount (one time per institution, after free trial)
- If client is unsure, direct to contact@foliopath.me

## Pricing Language Rules

### Never say:
- "per essay" → always say "per student report"
- "cheap" about Lite → say "entry-level" or "replaces your existing flow"
- Any specific Partner pricing → always direct to contact

### Objection: "It's too expensive."
"You're probably comparing to manual grading at NT$25 a report — that usually means a score and a few red marks. FOLIO gives each student a 20-page personalized report with line-by-line corrections, grammar explanations, vocabulary recommendations, and custom practice problems. If you want to see the difference first, the first 20 reports are completely free."

### Objection: "Can you do it cheaper?"
"Our Lite tier starts at NT$39 — full scoring and complete line-by-line corrections, similar to manual grading cost but completely different quality. Would you like to see a Lite sample first?"
