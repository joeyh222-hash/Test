## Official Grading Criteria (四項評分)

All scoring strictly follows the official 大考中心 GSAT English composition rubric:

| Dimension | Max | What it evaluates |
|---|---|---|
| 內容 Content | 5 | On-topic, sufficient detail |
| 組織 Organization | 5 | Paragraph structure, coherence, transitions |
| 文法句構 Grammar & Sentence Structure | 5 | Grammatical accuracy, sentence variety and maturity |
| 字彙拼字 Vocabulary & Spelling | 5 | Word choice precision, vocabulary range, spelling |

Total: 20 points. Each dimension scored independently.

## Key Report Sections (Standard)

### 1. Scoring Block
Total score + four sub-scores, each with a specific explanation of why the student received that score. Not generic template language.

### 2. Line-by-Line Corrections (逐字逐句訂正)
Two-tier markup system:
- **Tier 1 — Grammar/spelling errors:** ~~wrong~~[correct] → red strikethrough + blue bracket
- **Tier 2 — Style improvements:** __original__[better] → purple underline + blue bracket
Rule: surgical edits only. Max ~6 words per marked span. Student's sentence structure preserved.

### 3. Specific Revision Suggestions (Standard only)
Three sub-sections:
- Paragraph structure diagnosis with 3-level example sentences (basic → upgraded → high-score)
- Vocabulary correction by category (noun plurality, word form, collocations, Chinglish)
- Grammar focus corrections by category (gerunds, connectives, relative clauses, run-ons, conditionals)

### 4. Extended Materials (Standard only)
- Upgraded vocabulary (3–4 advanced words relevant to the essay topic)
- Universal sentence frameworks (3 transferable frameworks for comparison essays)
- Vocabulary ladder (2 sets of 3-tier upgrades: basic → intermediate → advanced)

### 5. Examiner Commentary (閱卷評語)
150–220 word prose in Traditional Chinese. Written from examiner's perspective. Must: open with specific strengths, cite actual student sentences, close with 1–2 actionable next steps. Not a template — every report independently generated.

### 6. Personalized Practice Exercises (Standard only)
2 exercises based on the student's specific weaknesses:
- Exercise 1: Same essay type as the original (reinforce the weak skill)
- Exercise 2: Related but different writing type
Each includes English prompt, Chinese explanation, focus point, transferable vocabulary, sentence frameworks.

### 7. AI Writing Detection (Standard only)
Flags: overly polished paragraph structure, anomalously advanced collocations, templated sentence patterns, lack of personal detail, inconsistent English proficiency across sections. Output is signal strength assessment — final judgment belongs to the teacher.

|---|---|

## Scoring Consistency

- Same essay processed twice: score variance ≤ ±1 point
- Four dimensions scored independently
- Advanced vocabulary used correctly = counted as strength, not penalized
- If essay prompt is missing: grading proceeds, commentary notes approximate on-topic assessment
