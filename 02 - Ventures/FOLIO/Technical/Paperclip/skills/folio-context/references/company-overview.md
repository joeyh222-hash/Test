# FOLIO — Company Overview

## What It Is

FOLIO is an AI-powered essay grading platform built specifically for Taiwan's GSAT (大學學測) English composition exam. It automates essay evaluation while staying aligned with official exam standards, delivering detailed personalized feedback reports back to cram schools and teachers within 3–5 business days.

Cram schools scan and submit student essays. FOLIO digitizes, grades, and generates personalized reports. Reports are returned as PDFs — no software installation or workflow changes required for the client.

## The Problem It Solves

Teachers don't have time to give meaningful feedback on dozens of essays. Students receive scores and red marks but don't know why they lost points or how to improve. FOLIO handles the mechanical grading so teachers can focus on teaching, and every student gets the detailed, exam-standard guidance they need.

> "A teacher's passion and empathy cannot be automated, but mechanical grading can." — Joey Huang, Co-founder

## Target Customers

- **Cram schools (補習班)** — primary customer. Streamline workflow, differentiate service offering, give parents visible proof of progress.
- **English teachers** — reclaim time from mechanical grading, focus on real teaching.
- **High school students** — detailed, individualized feedback aligned to exam standards.
- **Parents** — tangible progress documentation they can actually read.

## How It Works

1. School scans and submits student essays (email or agreed method)
2. FOLIO runs OCR, grades each essay, generates a personalized PDF report per student
3. Reports returned within 3–5 business days as a ZIP file
4. Client distributes reports to students

## Key Differentiators

- Official GSAT exam-standard alignment — not generic AI feedback
- Taiwan-focused design and pedagogy
- Zero operational burden on client schools
- Consistent, cross-teacher evaluation standards (no score drift)
- ~20-page personalized learning document per student, not just a score
