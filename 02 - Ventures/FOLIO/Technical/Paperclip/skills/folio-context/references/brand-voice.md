# FOLIO — Brand Voice & Messaging

## The Core Frame (StoryBrand)

The customer is the hero. FOLIO is the guide. Every communication puts the teacher or director at the center — FOLIO is just the tool that helps them succeed.

Never make FOLIO the hero. Never lead with technology. Always lead with the customer's problem.

---

## Target Personas

### Persona 1 — The Overloaded Teacher
- Who: Cram school English teacher, grading 20–50 essays every mock exam cycle
- Wants: Stop spending nights on grading. Put energy back into real teaching.
- Aspires to be: The teacher whose students actually improve, and who knows why.
- Inner feeling: "I work this hard and it feels like it's not making a difference."

### Persona 2 — The Director Who Wants Differentiation
- Who: Cram school director or academic coordinator. Responsible for enrollment, reputation, parent satisfaction.
- Wants: A differentiator that makes parents actively recommend their English writing class.
- Aspires to be: The school that other schools want to imitate.
- Inner feeling: "We need something that makes us stand out — something parents can see."

### Persona 3 — The Teacher Who Cares About Learning
- Who: A committed teacher who suspects the current system isn't producing real improvement.
- Wants: Students to understand their mistakes, not just receive a score.
- Aspires to be: A teacher who measures success by student transformation, not grading volume.

---

## The Problem We Name

### External (what they'd say out loud):
- "Too many papers, not enough time"
- "Students get scores but don't learn"
- "Different teachers grade differently — no consistency"

### Internal (what actually makes them act):
- "I work so hard and it feels useless"
- "I'm not sure I'm actually helping students"
- "This job has become administration, not teaching"

### Philosophical (why the status quo is wrong):
- A teacher's wellbeing shouldn't be the price of good student feedback
- Every student deserves to know WHY they lost points, not just HOW MANY
- A teacher's expertise should be used for teaching, not processing paper

## FOLIO's Authority

- Scoring built directly on official 大考中心 GSAT rubric — completely adopted, not adapted or approximated
- Founded by a 政大 English department student who tutored GSAT students for 2+ years
- Designed from inside the classroom, not from a codebase

**Never say:** "aligned with" or "similar to" official standards
**Always say:** "比照" (modeled after) or "完全採用" (completely adopts) the official 大考中心 rubric

---

## Tone Guidelines

- Warm, sincere, specific — not sales-pitch energy
- Lead with a concrete observation, not a rhetorical question
- State the structural problem, don't blame the teacher
- When describing the product, be factual and specific — let the details do the selling
- Low-pressure CTAs — always offer the free trial as the first step

**Avoid:**
- Overly punchy standalone sentences ("This changes everything.")
- Rhetorical questions as openers ("Have you ever wondered...?")
- Vague enthusiasm ("Amazing results!")
- Jargon like "AI-powered" as the lead — lead with what it does for the customer

## Key Messages by Audience

### To teachers (pain: time):
"English teachers grading 20–30 essays per mock exam, spending 15–20 minutes on each — that's an entire weekend. This isn't a teacher efficiency problem. The system structurally doesn't allow for individualized feedback at scale."

### To teachers (pain: learning gap):
"During FOLIO's development, we talked with many cram school teachers and students. We kept seeing the same thing: students glance at their score, look at the red marks, put the essay in their bag. Same problems next time. Not because they don't try — because nothing in that feedback told them what to do differently."

### To directors (pain: differentiation):
"The value of an essay writing class is hard for parents to see. The teacher graded it, the student got it back — and then what? A page full of red marks doesn't show a parent what their child learned. FOLIO gives every student a 20-page report parents can actually read."

## Things FOLIO Never Says

- "四維度評分" → always "四項評分"
- "對齊官方標準" → always "比照" or "完全採用"
- "per essay" → always "per student report"
- "cheap" about Lite → "entry-level" or "replaces your existing flow"
- "AI will replace teachers" → "FOLIO handles mechanical grading so teachers can focus on what can't be automated"
