## Company Name Backups

In priority order if Stackr is unavailable:

1. **Modl** — "modular" stripped down. Clean, minimal, modern.
2. **Blok** — building block. Short, bold. Blok Cooling, Blok Power, Blok Grip all work.
3. **Klik** — the sound/feel of snapping into place. Short, fun, global.
4. **Nexo** — from "nexus" (connection point). Suggests a connected ecosystem.
5. **Forma** — Latin for "form/shape." Premium feel, expansion-friendly.
6. **SnapKit** — snap-on + kit. Very clear for Kickstarter audiences.
7. **Orbis** — Latin for "ring/circle," referencing MagSafe's circular form.
8. **Corelink** — a core device you link things onto. Signals platform thinking.
9. **Modul** — one step away from "modular." European-tech feel.
10. **Stackr** ← primary choice

All to be paired with **Inc.**

## First Product Name

**Chosen: Glacius**

The first product — MagSafe water-cooling device for iPhone.

**Why Glacius over the alternatives:**
- Glacius has a premium, almost mythological feel — strong for a flagship product
- Kryos was a close second but sounds like "Cry" in casual speech, which is awkward for a consumer product
- Frostr and CryoSnap are great brand names but better suited as company names than product names
